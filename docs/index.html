Networking Rehearsal

Practise a first professional conversation with your own ChatGPT, the way you would practise speaking a new language. It plays the person you were just introduced to. You do the talking. Then it tells you what to change.

Start in ChatGPT

About ten minutes, in your own language, by text or voice. No account and nothing to install.

It stays in your chat

  • Your rehearsal happens inside your own ChatGPT chat and stays there.
  • Nothing you write, and nothing about you, is sent to us or to anyone else. We never see it.
  • This site is one page of instructions that ChatGPT reads. It has no account, no cookies and no analytics, and it receives nothing back.
  • The instructions tell ChatGPT not to search the web for you or for anyone, and not to contact anyone.
  • The other person is played by ChatGPT. Nobody real is contacted or informed.

Your chat is kept by ChatGPT like any other chat of yours, under its own terms. You can delete it there at any time.

How it goes

  1. Your profileChatGPT drafts three short answers about you from what it already knows. You correct and approve them.
  2. Your matchYou choose who to meet (a possible customer, someone a step ahead of you, someone who can open a door, or anyone you describe) and their mood.
  3. The introductionYou see who they are, what they bring you, what you bring them and how to open.
  4. The conversationYou speak for yourself. ChatGPT only plays the match, for a few minutes.
  5. The debriefWhat worked, what to change, and one of your lines said better. Then go again.
