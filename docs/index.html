Networking Rehearsal

Practise a first professional conversation with your own ChatGPT, the way you would practise speaking a new language. It plays the person you were just introduced to. You do the talking. Then it tells you what to change.

Start in ChatGPT

About ten minutes, in your own language, by text or voice. No account and nothing to install.

It stays in your chat

Nothing you write is sent to us or to anyone else. Your rehearsal lives only in your own ChatGPT chat, like any other chat of yours.

How it goes

  1. Profile. ChatGPT drafts three short answers about you; you approve them.
  2. Match. You choose who to meet and their mood.
  3. Introduction. Who they are, why you two should talk, how to open.
  4. Conversation. You speak for yourself; ChatGPT plays the match.
  5. Debrief. What worked and what to change. Then go again.
