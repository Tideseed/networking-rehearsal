Networking Rehearsal

Practise a first professional conversation with your own assistant, the way you would practise speaking a new language. A training simulation between you and your own assistant, which plays your match: someone you have just been introduced to. It drafts your profile, you choose who the match is, you carry the conversation, and it tells you what to change. Version 0.8.1.
